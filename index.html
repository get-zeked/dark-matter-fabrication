Questions

    

      
What can you print?
Most things: decor, props, working parts, prototypes and gifts. Pieces larger than the printer are printed in sections and joined.

      
Do I need a 3D file?
No. A photo, a sketch or a description is enough to start. If you have a file, send it and we'll check that it will print.

      
How much does it cost?
Price depends on size, material and print time. We send a quote first, and nothing prints until you approve it.

      
How long does it take?
It depends on the piece and how busy we are. Your quote includes an estimated date.

      
Can I buy ready-made prints?
Not yet. A shop is planned. For now, everything is made to order.
